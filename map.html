Nine storms, 67 counties. Fill the counties with the outage dose, the rain, the cell network, the silence index, the reimbursed emergency work or the completed mitigation, and scale the circles by the releases. Hover for the numbers; select a county to pin its nine-storm record.

Customer-hours per customer is the average number of hours each customer in the county was without power from the landfall day to day 14. The silence index is the log of observed over model-fitted releases; a large negative value is a county that filed far less than its outage predicts.
