One storm at a time: the daily sewer releases by release start, the share of customers without power, and the cell sites out of service from the FCC's daily reports. Drag the day or press play to move the county map through the event.

The FCC's reporting area narrows as a storm passes, so the cell series is drawn as a share of the storm's largest reporting area; a county leaves the table when the FCC drops it, not when its outage ends. Release starts are dates, so the day of a release is known but not the hour.
